Our Heritage & Craft

A Passion Born from Ocean Waves & Charcoal Fire

Founded by Master Chef Osman Al-Mansoor, our restaurant brings together the rich bounties of the Pacific waters and the time-honored tradition of wood-fired open hearth grilling.

We source all seafood directly from certified sustainable coastal fishermen every morning. Every marinade is prepared from scratch with whole herbs, sea salt, and smoked chilies.

🌊 Fresh Catch Daily

Delivered daily at dawn directly from Harbor Bay docks.

🔥 Oak Wood Charcoal

High-heat white oak char searing for unmatched flavor depth.

🧈 Signature Cajun Butter

Artisanal infused garlic butter blend crafted in-house.

🍷 Coastal Ambiance
